I just finished my capstone: a full-stack Hotel Management & Room Booking System. 🏨

Three roles (Guest, Receptionist, Admin), real-time room availability, no double bookings even for overlapping dates, dynamic weekend/seasonal pricing, PDF invoices, reviews and an analytics dashboard.

Stack: Node.js, Express, SQLite, JWT, vanilla JS, Chart.js, Jest and Playwright tests.
The trickiest part was the date-overlap logic. One formula (newIn < existingOut AND newOut > existingIn) inside a database transaction solved it.
